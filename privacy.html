Privacy policy

Tallyboard app. Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: your events and results stay on your device, or in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. There are no accounts and no ads, and we have no server. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, never names, results or any text you typed.

What Tallyboard does

Tallyboard scores amateur contests: groups, participants, results, standings and exports. The ready rules are examples and not an official rulebook.

What you enter

Event names, dates and places, group names, participants’ names, clubs, start numbers and optionally their year of birth, and the results. You enter participants’ names yourself: you are responsible for having their agreement and for removing them on request.

Where the data is

In the app’s storage on your device. Device backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you turn it on) may include the app’s data, according to your system settings.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your data is stored in your private iCloud (CloudKit, an Apple service) and appears on your devices with the same Apple ID. We have no access to it. The setting is off by default and takes effect after the app restarts.

Exports and backups

You create PDF and CSV files, podium images and .tallyboard backups yourself and share them as you choose (AirDrop, Mail, Messages, Files). After sharing, the sender and the recipient are responsible for further use.

Tips

Optional tips (Bronze, Silver, Gold) are one-off App Store purchases handled by Apple. The app never sees your payment details or Apple ID, and tips unlock nothing.

Usage statistics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They receive anonymous events (an event was created, participants were added with their number, a result was saved, an export was made with its format, an event was finished, the introduction was completed), the device model, the system and app version, the country and a random installation identifier. We do not send event names, participants’ names, results or any text you typed, we do not use the advertising identifier and we do not track you across apps. You can switch the usage statistics off in Settings. Crash reports contain a technical trace and are used only to fix bugs.

Controller

The controller of the data collected by the app (usage statistics and crash reports) is IdeaApps. The basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in developing the app and fixing bugs. You have the right to access, rectify and erase the data, to object and to complain to a supervisory authority. Write to us to exercise these rights.

Deleting data

Deleting an event removes it, with its groups, participants and results, from the device (and from iCloud if sync is on). Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device.

Children

The app is not intended for children. Organisers who enter children’s data (for example the year of birth) are responsible for having the guardians’ agreement.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, we will update this page and say so in the app.

Contact
