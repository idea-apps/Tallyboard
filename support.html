How do I create an event?

Tap + on the event list, name the event, pick a kind of contest and add groups. Each group has its own tries, way of counting and penalties.

How do I add participants?

Open the event, then Participants. Add them one by one (Save keeps the form open for the next person), paste a list or import a CSV. The start number is filled in for you.

How do I run the contest?

Open a group: the app shows who is next. Tap Start, use the stopwatch (also the volume-up button on iPhone), add penalties and save. The next participant appears by itself.

How do I finish the event?

Open the event and tap Finish event. The results are marked as final and entering is locked. Swipe the event in the list, or tap Reopen event, to continue.

How do I export results?

In an event use the export button: PDF, CSV, a podium image or a .tallyboard backup. Import a backup in Settings.

How do I turn on iCloud sync?

Settings → Export and backups → iCloud sync. You need an iCloud account on the device. Quit and reopen the app afterwards.
